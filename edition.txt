OPEN INTEREST / Daily brief, format v2. Thursday, 26 November 2026.

LEAD: Ukraine's parliament adopts a virtual-asset bill at second reading.

On 25 November 2026, Ukraine's parliament adopted a virtual-asset bill that also changes tax law. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/summari_pz/277070.html] It assigns oversight to the securities commission and central bank and sets customer-asset protections. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/summari_pz/277070.html]

Draft law 10225-d amends Ukraine's Tax Code and other legislation, according to the Verkhovna Rada. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/summari_pz/277070.html] It divides supervision between the National Securities and Stock Market Commission and the National Bank of Ukraine, and sets rules for market participants and their operations. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/summari_pz/277070.html]

Parliament says the bill requires transparency and separation of client assets from company assets, describing them as investor protections. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/summari_pz/277070.html] It also addresses taxes on virtual-asset transactions and a path for holders to legalize assets they acquired earlier. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/summari_pz/277070.html] The parliament says the law aims to let innovation develop transparently while accounting for financial stability and state security. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/summari_pz/277070.html]

A 24 October 2024 parliamentary review said the 2022 Virtual Assets law had not entered into force. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/print/254878.html] It called tax-code changes necessary to create conditions for a legal market. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/print/254878.html]

On 23 November 2026, the finance committee reported 2,562 amendments before the second-reading vote: it accepted 280, partly accepted 27, incorporated 112 editorially and rejected 2,143. [https://komfinbank.rada.gov.ua/uploads/documents/34624.pdf]

The bill tracker records adoption on 25 November and lists the status as prepared for signature. [https://itd.rada.gov.ua/billinfo/Bills/Card/56271] It provides no effective date. [https://itd.rada.gov.ua/billinfo/Bills/Card/56271]

The parliament's summary gives no eligibility test, tax rate, filing deadline or evidence requirements for legalizing earlier assets. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/summari_pz/277070.html] It does not say which authority handles each type of virtual-asset activity or whether regulations must follow. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/summari_pz/277070.html]

[NEWS] Braiins reports Bitcoin mining traffic redirected through forged routes.
On 25 November 2026, Braiins described four episodes on 20 November when attackers rerouted miners' connections to an imposter server. [https://braiins.com/blog/bitcoin-hashrate-hijacking-unencrypted-stratum-v1]
The company says 1,050 to 1,600 mining sites lost their usual pool connection in each episode, which lasted 6 to 49 minutes. [https://braiins.com/blog/bitcoin-hashrate-hijacking-unencrypted-stratum-v1]
Its report gives no attacker identity or total for diverted mining rewards. [https://braiins.com/blog/bitcoin-hashrate-hijacking-unencrypted-stratum-v1]

[NEWS] Switzerland approves 57 partners for crypto-asset reporting.
On 25 November 2026, Switzerland's Federal Council approved 57 jurisdictions for automatic crypto-asset information exchange starting in 2028. [https://www.admin.ch/en/newnsb/hy3YlIU2OxQn]
The government says exchange depends on a partner's request and compliance with OECD standards, including confidentiality and data security. [https://www.admin.ch/en/newnsb/hy3YlIU2OxQn]
It will assess compliance before exchanging information with each jurisdiction. [https://www.admin.ch/en/newnsb/hy3YlIU2OxQn]

[TECH] Potuz argues that faster Ethereum broadcasts could give time back to the protocol.
In a 1 September 2026 essay, Ethereum researcher Potuz says time spent sending messages can instead support larger blocks, shorter slots or resilience. [https://www.potuz.net/posts/hegota-faster-blocks/]
He describes chunked broadcast, which splits a payload into pieces that peers can send in parallel. [https://www.potuz.net/posts/hegota-faster-blocks/]
Potuz warns that faster propagation adds protocol complexity and says patent concerns complicate some coding methods. [https://www.potuz.net/posts/hegota-faster-blocks/]

[TECH] Provenance patched a marker-account bug that exposed live assets.
Trail of Bits researchers Paweł Płatek and Denys Pakizh reported in August 2026 that a bug let users grant themselves admin control over Provenance marker accounts without holding the asset. [https://blog.trailofbits.com/2026/08/25/state-divergence-enables-unauthorized-access/]
The bug affected 82 markers representing live financial assets on mainnet and versions before 1.28.0. [https://blog.trailofbits.com/2026/08/25/state-divergence-enables-unauthorized-access/]
The researchers say a mitigation shipped on 1 May 2026 and a full fix shipped on 8 June 2026. [https://blog.trailofbits.com/2026/08/25/state-divergence-enables-unauthorized-access/]
